The Studio

What Simply Create KC Is All About

For everyone, literally.

You don't need to know how to draw. You don't need experience. You just need to show up. Cayla guides every session step-by-step so that anyone — regardless of background or skill — leaves with something they're proud of.

A space to actually create.

The studio is set up to make creating feel comfortable, not intimidating. The vibe is relaxed, the instruction is clear, and the goal is to leave with a finished piece and a good feeling about making it.
