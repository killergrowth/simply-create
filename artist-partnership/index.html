Partnership Opportunities

What a partnership could look like.

We're open to collaboration in a lot of forms. Here are some ways we've thought about working with other artists:

  • → Guest instructor for a class or series
  • → Featured artwork in the studio or shop
  • → Co-hosted events or pop-ups
  • → Social media or content collaborations
  • → Something entirely different — pitch us

No formal requirements. If you're a working artist in the KC area who's passionate about their craft and the community, reach out.
