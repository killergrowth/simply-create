About Simply Create

Art is for everyone.
We're here to prove it.

Simply Create KC is a creative studio where you come as you are and leave with something you made yourself. Whether it's a date night, a girls' trip, or just a night out for yourself — our guided painting classes are designed to be approachable, fun, and genuinely creative.

About

Hi, I'm Cayla.

I started Simply Create KC because I believe everyone has a creative side — it just needs the right space to show up. I'm a Kansas City-based artist and instructor who loves helping people discover what they're capable of making.

Whether you're picking up a brush for the first time or you've been painting for years, you're welcome here.
